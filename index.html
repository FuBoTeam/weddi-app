Weddi

A live wedding greeting wall your guests post to from their phones

Weddi is a wedding-greeting web app. Guests send messages and a photo to the bride and groom from their phone browser, and every greeting shows up live on a photo wall on the venue screen. When it's time, draw lottery winners from everyone who sent a greeting.

See a demo

The demo opens a sample event. The in-app interface is mostly in Chinese.

How it works

  1. Share your event QR code (or link)

    Guests scan the event QR code, which is basically a link, and open it in their phone browser. There is no app to install and no account to create.

  2. Guests write a greeting

    Guests enter a name or nickname, a message, and a photo (pick one of the couple's or upload their own).

  3. It appears on the big screen

    The wall shows new greetings first, each for as long as it takes to read.

Features

  • A live greeting wall on the venue screen

    Every greeting appears on the venue screen with its photo, over a collage of your pictures. New greetings go up first; in quieter moments the wall cycles back through earlier ones.

  • Greetings from any phone, no app to install

    Guests open your event link in their phone browser, type their name and a message, and send it. There is no app to install.

  • Pick a photo or upload one

    Each greeting comes with a picture: guests choose one of your photos or upload their own, which is resized on the phone before it is sent.

  • Paced for reading

    Each greeting stays on screen for as long as it takes to read, based on its length and language, so short notes and long messages both get their moment.

  • A wedding lottery drawn from the greetings

    Everyone who sends a greeting gets a card in the draw. Shuffle the deck on the big screen, then flip cards to reveal the winners.

  • Made for your day

    Your event gets its own page with your names in the title and your photos throughout the form and the wall.

Frequently asked questions

What is Weddi?

Weddi is a wedding-greeting web app. Guests send a message and a photo to the couple from their phone, each greeting appears live on a photo wall on the venue screen, and the couple can draw lottery winners from everyone who sent a greeting.

Do guests need to download an app or create an account?

No. Guests scan the event QR code (which is basically a link) or open the event link in their phone's web browser, type a name and a message, choose a photo, and send it.

What appears on the big screen?

A live wall that shows each greeting with its photo over a collage of the couple's pictures. New greetings are shown first, and in quieter moments the wall cycles back through earlier ones.

How long does each greeting stay on screen?

Long enough to read it. Weddi estimates the reading time from the message's length and language, with a minimum of about five seconds, so short notes and long messages both get their moment.

Can guests add photos?

Yes. Every greeting comes with a picture: guests pick one of the couple's photos or upload their own, and uploads are resized on the phone before sending.

How does the wedding lottery work?

Everyone who sends a greeting gets a card in the draw. On the big screen you shuffle the deck, then flip cards to reveal the winners.

Is the app in English or Chinese?

This page is in English. The greeting form, wall, and lottery screens are mostly in Traditional Chinese, and guests can write greetings in any language.

How do we get Weddi for our wedding?

Each event is set up with its own page, title, and photos. Contact us first at contact@weddi.app; the price is not decided yet.
